Prologue

I was in my room playing videogames. I felt like something was wrong. Somebody called me, but I didn't recognize the voice... Who is in my home? Almost like the voice was in my head? ZAP* A lighting struck me and suddenly I was falling from the sky above the clouds.

This game is meant for people over the age of 12. Your decisions will lead the story and characters. Meaning there is no going back once the decision is made. Movement will happen with a keyboard. Which you will need to type in when given the chance. The available directions will be displayed on the screen. The combat is randomized. Remember: better damage means lower chance to hit. Combat is pressing 'Enter' and hoping you will hit. There will always be a checkpoint before a fight. Choose a path and see where it leads... Good luck!
